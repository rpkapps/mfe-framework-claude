---
title: 'Report an error'
description: 'Record a failure you caught, and see what happens to one you did not.'
---

`telemetry.error` reports a failure without throwing it or handling it. A failure you do not catch
costs your part of the page and nothing else. Apps and Widgets.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### 1. Report a failure you caught

```tsx title="examples/lab/src/routes/telemetry.tsx"
try {
  await save(draft)
} catch (error) {
  telemetry.error(error, { step: 'save' })
}
```

The record carries the error's name, its message and your attributes. It is a report, not a rethrow.

</Step>
<Step>
### 2. Let the boundary take the rest

```tsx title="examples/lab/src/routes/failure.tsx"
function Exploder(): ReactNode {
  throw new Error('The lab threw on purpose, from inside a mounted App.')
}
```

A throw you do not catch reaches the boundary around your mount. The reader sees the message and a
Retry button in your region. The shell chrome stays, and every other App on the page stays
reachable.

</Step>
<Step>
### 3. Give a rendered Widget its own fallback

```tsx title="examples/operations/src/routes/index.tsx"
<AlertPanel
  alertId="a-1001"
  fallback={({ error, retry }) => (
    <div role="alert">
      <p>{error.message}</p>
      <Button onPress={retry}>Retry</Button>
    </div>
  )}
/>
```

When you render somebody else's Widget, `fallback` is your chance to keep the rest of your screen
working. `retry` is a fresh attempt rather than a re-render of the broken tree.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Check it works

In a test, `environment.telemetry.logs('error')` holds the report, with `error.type` set from the
error's name. In the shell, open `/lab/failure` and throw on purpose. Your region shows the
failure, the header keeps working, and the application finder still moves you elsewhere. Framework
failures, such as a storage or command failure, are reported to the shell for you before they are
thrown.

## Errors

<Callout type="error" title="dispose/failure, in development">

```text
lab failed to record a error log: expected telemetry only while the mount is live, received a
telemetry call arrived after the mount was disposed.
```

**Fix.** The work that failed outlived your mount. Cancel it with `useMfeSignal()`, or check the
signal before you report.

</Callout>

<Callout type="error" title="mount/failure, outside a mount">

```text
<unmounted> failed to call useTelemetry: expected a component rendered inside an App or Widget
mount, received a component rendered outside any mount.
```

**Fix.** Move the call into a component your App or Widget renders. In a route callback, read
`context.mfe.telemetry` instead.

</Callout>
